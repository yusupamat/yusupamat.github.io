Mean displacement map of ENVISAT-ASAR data from 2003 to 2010. The mean displacement velocity map of the SBAS-InSAR method. Positive (negative) values indicates the ground close to (away from) the satellite. 

The maximum deformation rate attributed to subsurface water injection for the period from 2003 to 2010 was up to approximately 33 mm/year in the line of sight (LOS) direction.
