Based on a NASA - National Aeronautics and Space Administration image discovered by Huub Eggen, we identified a sand dune field more than 30 km long within the Burqin–Haba River–Jimunai Desert, located near the borders of the Xinjiang region of northwestern China, Mongolia, Russia and Kazakhstan, WNW of Burqin.
The Irtysh River, together with its associated wetlands and riparian vegetation, flows from its headwaters in the Altay Mountains through Burqin toward Siberia. It is the only river in China whose waters ultimately reach the Arctic Ocean.
The tan-colored linear dunes at the center of the image were formed by the coalescence of mobile barchan (crescent-shaped) dunes, reaching heights of approximately 50–100 m. In the southeastern part of the image, distinctive star dunes are visible.
