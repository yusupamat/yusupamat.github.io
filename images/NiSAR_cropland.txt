This may look like abstract art, but it’s agricultural data taken by the NISAR satellite.
Collected over the Maize Triangle in South Africa, each color in the data provides clues about crop types and how they changed over the growing season.
In the image, green indicates a vegetated area, red represents an unvegetated surface, and blue indicates how quickly a vegetated area changed during the season.The data helps scientists track how crops grow, how irrigation affects the land, and how land-use changes across large areas.
