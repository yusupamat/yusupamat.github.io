A lot of the ICEYE team are doing their best to support the terrible situation in Iceland. When this SAR image came across my desk it was too pretty not to share. I was really surprised that the ocean is clearly not frozen just off the coast. (unlike here in Helsinki). In this image, taken a few hours ago, you can clearly see how the lava is encroaching on the village of Grindavik, Iceland. There are also several other interesting features such as power lines, RADAR layover and lots of specular reflections from buildings in the village.
